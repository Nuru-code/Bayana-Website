The Bayana Standard

Most compliance infrastructure was built to be sold, not to be trusted. Bayana was built for the organizations where a single failure has consequences that are regulatory, reputational and irreversible. We believe that precision and speed are not a tradeoff. That agentic commerce and institutional compliance are not in contention. That NURU AI is the category-defining, first-principles build of the fourth industrial revolution of institutional-grade infrastructure.

I
Pre-Transaction Control

Every screening decision is made before a single dollar moves. Not a retrospective flag after funds have settled, but a cryptographically sealed HOLD instruction issued pre-broadcast. No funds are ever at risk of non-compliant movement.

II
Machine-Speed Identity

In Phase 1 sandbox testing, NURU AI 3.0 achieved a 13ms median decision latency across KYC, KYB, and KYA verification, compared to an industry standard of 300ms. Zero-knowledge credentials verify once and travel everywhere, with no PII stored centrally.

III
Non-Custodial

Bayana never holds keys. Non-custodial MPC is enforced at the infrastructure level through AWS Nitro Enclave hardware isolation. It is not a policy or a promise; it is an architectural constraint that no operator can override.
